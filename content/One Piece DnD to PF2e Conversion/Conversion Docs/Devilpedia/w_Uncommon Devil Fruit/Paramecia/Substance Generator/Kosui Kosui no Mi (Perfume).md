##### Kosui Kosui no Mi (Perfume)
*Devil Fruit, Uncommon*

**Type.** Substance Generator Paramecia

**Fruit appearance.** A violet, delicate-looking fruit with a soft, velvety texture. It has pink, wavy patterns covering its surface and exudes a sweet, alluring aroma.

**Description.** Grants the user the ability to produce and control various perfumes with different effects, making them a Perfume Human. The user can release these perfumes in various ways to affect their surroundings and others.

**Sea Weakness.** You are weak to water and seastone.

**Perfume Resistance.** You are immune to the effects of your own perfumes and any other perfumes or scents.

**Pleasant Aroma.** You learn the friends cantrip, no material components required.

**Scented Aura.** As an action, you can activate an aura of pleasant perfume that extends 5 feet from you in all directions for up to 1 minute. Any creature that enters or starts its turn in the area can choose to be charmed by you until the start of their next turn. The charmed creature regards you as a friendly acquaintance. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to your Scented Aura for 24 hours. You can deactivate the aura as a bonus action.

**Spells (Perfume Techniques).** This devil fruit power has 5 charges. You can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it without needing to expend material components: Charming Perfume (Charm Person; 1 charge and can cast at higher levels by spending additional charges), Enchanting Perfume (as Suggestion; 2 charges), Hypnotizing Perfume (as Hypnotic Pattern, 3 charges). The devil fruit regains expended charges daily at dawn.

**Awakening (Enchanting Fragrance).** Once you've awakened your power, you can release a powerful fragrance into the air around you, allowing you to cast Mass Suggestion, no spell slots or material components required. After using this ability, you can't use it again for another 24 hours.
