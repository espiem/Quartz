##### Stinku Stinku no Mi (Stink)
*Devil Fruit, Uncommon*

**Type.** Body Enhancer Paramecia

**Fruit appearance.** A toxic green devil fruit that looks like a bumpy cloud, it's swirls are a darker shade of green.

**Description.** Grants the user the ability to produce and control foul odors, making them a Stinking Human. The user can release these odors in various ways to affect their surroundings and others.

**Sea Weakness.** You are weak to water and seastone.

**Stench Resistance.** You are immune to the effects of your own stink and any other stinking or nauseating effects.

**Skunk Spray.** You learn the poison spray cantrip.

**Stinking Aura.** As an action, you can activate an aura of stink that extends 5 feet from you in all directions for up to 1 minute. Any creature that enters or starts its turn in the area must make a Constitution saving throw against your Devil Fruit save DC or become poisoned until the start of their next turn. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to your Stinking Aura for 24 hours. You can deactivate the aura as a bonus action.

**Stink Bomb.** As an action, you can spend 1 devil fruit charge to create and hurl a stink bomb at a point within 60 feet. The bomb explodes in a 20-foot radius cloud of foul odor, which lingers for 1 minute. Any creature in the cloud must make a Constitution saving throw against your Devil Fruit save DC or be poisoned for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself with a successful save. On a successful saving throw, the creature is immune to your Stink Bomb for 24 hours.

**Spells (Stink Techniques).** This devil fruit power has 5 charges. You can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it without needing to expend material components: Ray of Sickness (1 charge, and can cast at higher levels by spending additional charges), or Stinking Cloud (3 charges). The devil fruit regains expended charges daily at dawn.

**Awakening (Foul Miasma).** Once you've awakened your power, you can use an action to release a miasma of stink in a 60-foot radius centered on you. The area in the radius acts as the Cloudkill spell, except you and your allies are immune to its effects, but cannot see through it. The miasma lasts for 1 minute or until you fall unconscious. After using this ability, you can't use it again for another 24 hours.
