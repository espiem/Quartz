##### Dansu Dansu no Mi (Dancing)
*Devil Fruit, Uncommon*

**Type.** Body Enhancer Paramecia

**Fruit appearance.** A black ball fruit that kind of looks like a disco ball, but instead of it being squares that reflect light, it's the swirls themselves that are tiny mirrors.

**Description.**  Allows the user to dance very well while forcing others to break into dance, which can be incredibly useful if you wish to create a popular band.

**Sea Weakness.** You are weak to water and seastone.

**Enchanted Rhythm.** You gain proficiency in the Performance skill, and gain advantage on checks made to dance. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against being slowed or immobilized.

**Dance Performance.** As an action, you can choose to perform one of the following dance styles and gain its benefits for up to 1 minute. You cannot perform multiple dances simultaneously. You must concentrate on maintaining these performances, as if concentrating on a spell:

- **Disco.** As you perform a lively disco dance, you exude an aura of energy in a 30-foot radius centered on you. You and your allies within the aura have advantage on Charisma-based skill checks.
- **Hip Hop.** With your hip hop performance, you channel raw energy. Your movement speed increases by 15 feet.
- **Tango.** As you perform a passionate tango, you can choose one willing creature within 5 feet of you to be your dance partner. While you maintain the tango, both you and your partner gain a +2 bonus to AC, and you can move through other creatures' spaces without provoking opportunity attacks. If your partner is separated from you by more than 5 feet, the effect ends for both of you.
- **Waltz.** Your graceful waltz mesmerizes those around you. As long as you maintain the waltz, you have advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks, and hostile creatures within 30 feet of you have disadvantage on saving throws against being charmed.
- **Ballet.** Your elegant ballet dance enchants the area around you. While you maintain the ballet, you gain a flying speed of 30 feet, which only lasts during your turn, after which you fall to the ground if you are midair.

**Spells (Dancing Techniques).** This devil fruit power has 5 charges. You can expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it without needing to expend material components: Uncontrollable Breakdance (as Tasha's Hideous Laughter; 1 charge), Flawless Footwork (as Kinetic Jaunt; 2 charges), or Hypnotic Dance (as Hypnotic Pattern, forcing creatures to dance in place if they fail; 3 charges). The devil fruit regains expended charges daily at dawn.

**Awakening (Dance of Domination).** Once you've awakened your power, you can use an action to perform a mesmerizing dance in a 30-foot radius centered on you. Each creature of your choice in the area must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, creatures are affected by the Otto's Irresistable Dance spell. You can't use this action again for another 24 hours.
