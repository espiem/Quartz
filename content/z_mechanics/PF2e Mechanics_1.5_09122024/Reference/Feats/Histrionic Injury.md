# Histrionic Injury `pf2:r`
## Feat 5
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Mental]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Poppet]]

**Category** ancestry; 




**Frequency** once per hour

**Trigger** A creature you can see damages you with a melee Strike.

* * *

You respond with a startled look, dramatic pratfall, or theatrical arm-flailing that stymies your attacker. The attacker must succeed at a Will saving throw against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied|Stupefied 1]] for 1 round ([[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied|Stupefied 2]] on a critical failure).
