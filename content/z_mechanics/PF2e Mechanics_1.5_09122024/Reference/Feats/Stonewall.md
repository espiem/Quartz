### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Dwarf]][[earth]][[polymorph]]

**Category** ancestry; 




**Trigger** An enemy or hazard's effect hits you or you fail a Fortitude save against one.

The strength of stone overcomes you so strongly that it replaces your stout body. You become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Petrified|Petrified]] until the end of the current turn.
