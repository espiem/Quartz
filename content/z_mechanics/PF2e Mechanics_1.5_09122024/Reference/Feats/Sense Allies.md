# Sense Allies
## Feat 5
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Human]]

**Category** ancestry; 




Like many humans raised in a close-knit community, you have always been strongly attuned to the presence of others. Willing allies that you are aware of within 60 feet that would otherwise be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Undetected|Undetected]] by you are instead [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Hidden|Hidden]] from you. The flat check for you to target willing allies within 60 feet that are hidden from you is DC 5 Flat check instead of 11.
