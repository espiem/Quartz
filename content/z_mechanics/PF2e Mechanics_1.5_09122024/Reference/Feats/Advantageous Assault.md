# Advantageous Assault `pf2:1`
## Feat 6
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Fighter]][[press]]

**Category** class; 




After compromising your enemy's movement, you deliver a more deadly blow. Make a Strike against a creature that is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Grabbed|Grabbed]], [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone|Prone]], or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Restrained|Restrained]]. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage on this Strike equal to the number of weapon damage dice, or that number + 2 if you're wielding the weapon in two hands. The Strike gains the following failure effect.

**Failure** You deal damage to the target equal to the number of weapon damage dice, or that number + 2 if you're wielding the weapon in two hands. This damage has the same damage type as the weapon.
