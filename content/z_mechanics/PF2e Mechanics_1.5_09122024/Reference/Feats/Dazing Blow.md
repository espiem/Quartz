# Dazing Blow `pf2:1`
## Feat 6
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Fighter]][[press]]

**Category** class; 




**Requirements** You have a creature [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Grabbed|Grabbed]].

* * *

You pummel a held foe, hoping to stagger them. Make a melee Strike against a creature you have grabbed. The weapon damage from this Strike is bludgeoning damage. If the Strike hits, the creature must attempt a Fortitude check save against your class DC; this is an incapacitation effect.

* * *

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is [[Conditions/Stunned|Stunned 1]].

**Failure** The creature is [[Conditions/Stunned|Stunned 2]].

**Critical Failure** The creature is [[Conditions/Stunned|Stunned 3]].
