# Sure Feet
## Feat 1
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Halfling]]

**Category** ancestry; 




Whether keeping your balance or climbing, your feet easily find purchase. If you roll a success on an Acrobatics check to [[Actions/Balance|Balance]] or an Athletics check to[[Actions/Climb|Climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] when you attempt to Balance or Climb.
