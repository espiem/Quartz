The extent of your corruption devastates your foes' minds and reflexes. When you use [[Feats/Cruelty|Cruelty]], you can choose to make the target [[Conditions/Clumsy|Clumsy]] or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied|Stupefied]] instead, with the same condition value. Make the choice when you Cast the Spell (before you know the result of the target's save)
