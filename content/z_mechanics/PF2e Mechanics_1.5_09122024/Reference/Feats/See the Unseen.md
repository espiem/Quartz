# See the Unseen
## Feat 13
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Goloma]]

**Category** ancestry; 




You notice things that others can't see at all. While you're adjacent to an [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Undetected|Undetected]] creature of your level or lower, it is instead only [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Hidden|Hidden]] from you. You only need a successful DC 5 Flat check to target a hidden creature.

*Source: Pathfinder Lost Omens: The Mwangi Expanse*
