# Combat Grab `pf2:1`
## Feat 2
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Fighter]][[press]]

**Category** class; 




**Requirements** You have one hand free, and your target is within reach of that hand.

* * *

Using your prior attack to shift your opponent's guard, you take another swing and grab them. Make a melee Strike while keeping one hand free. If the Strike hits, you grab the target using your free hand. The creature remains [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Grabbed|Grabbed]] until the end of your next turn or until it [[Actions/Escape|Escapes]], whichever comes first.
