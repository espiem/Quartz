# Tenacious Endurance
## Feat 6
### [[archetype]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Emotion]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Mental]]

**Category** class; 



**Prerequisites** Beastmaster Dedication

You encourage your companion to refuse to yield, against even the mightiest of foes. When one of your animal companions is reduced to 0 Hit Points, if it isn't wounded, it can remain standing with 1 Hit Point and become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Wounded|Wounded 1]].
