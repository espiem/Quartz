# Scintillating Spell `pf2:1`
## Feat 16
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Concentrate]][[light]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Sorcerer]][[spellshape]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Wizard]]

**Category** class; 




Your spells become a radiant display of light and color. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that doesn't have the darkness trait, has no duration, and requires creatures to attempt a Reflex save, the spell explodes in a spray of scintillating lights, in addition to its other effects. Each creature that failed its Reflex save against the spell is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Dazzled|Dazzled]] for 1 round, and those who critically failed are instead [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] for 1 round.
