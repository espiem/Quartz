# Greater Despair
## Feat 12
### [[archetype]]

**Category** class; 



**Prerequisites** Mummy's Despair
* * *
You increase the frequency of [[Feats/Mummy's Despair|Mummy's Despair]] to once per 10 minutes instead of once per hour. A creature that critically fails its Will saving throw is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Paralyzed|Paralyzed]] for 1 round instead of [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 2]]. Mummy's Despair gains the incapacitation trait.

*Source: Pathfinder Book of the Dead*
