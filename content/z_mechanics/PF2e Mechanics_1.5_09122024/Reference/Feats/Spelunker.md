# Spelunker
## Feat 1
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Shisk]]

**Category** ancestry; 




Living underground, you have learned to find your way without landmarks or even solid ground. If you roll a success on an Survival check to [[Actions/Sense Direction|Sense Direction]] or an Athletics check to [[Actions/Climb|Climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] when you attempt to Climb.

*Source: Pathfinder Lost Omens: The Mwangi Expanse*
