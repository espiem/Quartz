# Toppling Dance
## Feat 13
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Ancestries/Halfling]]

**Category** ancestry; 



**Prerequisites** Dance Underfoot
* * *
While sharing a creature's space using Dance Underfoot, your melee weapons and unarmed attacks gain the trip trait, but only against the creature whose space you share. You can be in the same space as a Large or larger [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone|Prone]] creature, even if it's not your ally.
