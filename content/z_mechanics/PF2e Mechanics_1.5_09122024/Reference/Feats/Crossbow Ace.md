# Crossbow Ace `pf2:1`
## Feat 1
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Ranger]]

**Category** class; 




**Requirements** You are wielding a crossbow with reload 1 or higher.

* * *

Your deep understanding of the crossbow allows you to reload efficiently while moving yourself out of the line of return fire. Either [[Actions/Create a Diversion|Create a Diversion]] or [[Actions/Take Cover|Take Cover]], then Interact to reload. As normal, you must meet the requirements to Take Cover; you must be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone|Prone]], benefiting from cover, or near a feature that allows you to Take Cover.
