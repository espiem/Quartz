# Eternal Boost
## Feat 20
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Summoner]]

**Category** class; 




Your link with your eidolon is so intense that you continuously reinforce its abilities. You are permanently [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Quickened|Quickened]]; you can use your extra action only to cast _boost eidolon_ or _reinforce eidolon_.

While in exploration mode, you can declare that you are also continually casting _boost eidolon_ or _reinforce eidolon_ even while using a different exploration activity. If you do, even before your first turn in a combat encounter, that cantrip is active as if you had cast it on your previous turn.
