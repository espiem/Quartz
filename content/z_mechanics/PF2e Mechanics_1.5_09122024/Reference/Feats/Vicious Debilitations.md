# Vicious Debilitations
## Feat 10
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Rogue]]

**Prerequisites** ruffian racket; Debilitating Strike

The debilitations you dish out seriously impair your foes. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use Debilitating Strike.

*   **Debilitation** The target gains weakness 5 to your choice of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage. [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Feat_Feature Effects/Effect_ Vicious Debilitations|Effect: Vicious Debilitations]]
