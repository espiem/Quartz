# Anticipate Ambush
## Feat 6
### [[exploration]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Rogue]]

**Category** class; 



**Prerequisites** expert in Stealth
* * *
You keep watch for any [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Hidden|Hidden]] creatures or dangerous sites, moving at half your travel Speed. If you're Anticipating an Ambush at the start of an encounter, you roll a Perception check for initiative, and all enemies rolling Stealth for initiative take a –2 circumstance penalty to their Stealth checks.

*Source: Pathfinder Player Core*
