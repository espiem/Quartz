# Collateral Thrash
## Feat 16
### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Barbarian]][[rage]]

**Category** class; 



**Prerequisites** Thrash
* * *
You smack the foe you're thrashing into someone else. When you Thrash, choose another enemy adjacent to the [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Grabbed|Grabbed]] enemy. That enemy also takes your Thrash damage, with a Reflex check save against your class DC.
