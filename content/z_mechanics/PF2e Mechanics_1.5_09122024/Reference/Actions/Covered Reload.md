# Covered Reload `pf2:1`

### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/Classes/Gunslinger]]

**Category** offensive; 




You duck into a safe position or minimize your profile while reloading to make your next attack. Either [[Actions/Take Cover|Take Cover]] or attempt to [[Actions/Hide|Hide]], then Interact to reload. As normal, you must meet the requirements to Take Cover or Hide; you must be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone|Prone]], benefiting from cover, or near a feature that allows you to Take Cover, and you need to be benefiting from cover or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed|Concealed]] to a creature to Hide from that creature.

*Source: Pathfinder Guns & Gears*
