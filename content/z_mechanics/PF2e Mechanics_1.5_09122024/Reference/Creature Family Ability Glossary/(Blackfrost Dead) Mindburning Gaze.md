# (Blackfrost Dead) Mindburning Gaze `pf2:2`

### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Emotion]][[fear]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Occult]][[visual]]

**Category** offensive; 




The blackfrost dead fixes its terrifying gaze on a creature within 60 feet. The creature takes 1d4 mental per level of the blackfrost dead (minimum 2d4), with a Will check save. On a critical failure, the creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 1]].
