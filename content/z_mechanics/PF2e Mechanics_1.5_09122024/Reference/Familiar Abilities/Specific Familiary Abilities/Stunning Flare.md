# Stunning Flare `pf2:1`

### [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Fire]][[light]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Magical]]

**Category** familiar; 




**Frequency** once per 10 minutes;

* * *

**Effect** Your lantern wisp unleashes a large burst of flame, casting an unnaturally bright light. Each creature within a 15-foot emanation must attempt a Fortitude check save against your class DC or spell DC, whichever is higher, or be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Blinded|Blinded]] for 1 round and then [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Dazzled|Dazzled]] for 2 rounds after.
