# Athamasi

### 






You have a set of small secondary arms adapted for climbing and hanging from trees. You can't hold or retrieve objects with these limbs, but you can Climb or [[Actions/Grab an Edge|Grab an Edge]] even if one or both of your primary hands are full. You aren't [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] while Climbing.
