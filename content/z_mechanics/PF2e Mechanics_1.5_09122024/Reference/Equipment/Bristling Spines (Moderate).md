# Bristling Spines (Moderate)
## Item 3
### [[graft]][[invested]][[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Magical]]


**Price** 60 gp; 
**Bulk** 0; **Size** med
**Usage** implanted

Your exposed skin is covered in fine, needle-like hairs that you can flick into the eyes of enemies.

* * *

**Activate—Urticating Burst** `pf2:2` (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

**Effect** You flick tiny spines in a 15-foot cone, dealing 2d8 piercing damage to all creatures in the area with a DC 18 Reflex check save. On a critical failure, the creature is also [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Dazzled|Dazzled]] until the end of their next turn.

*Source: Pathfinder Howl of the Wild*
