### [uncommon](uncommon "Uncommon Rarity Trait")[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Magical]]

**Bulk** 0; **Size** med
**Usage** etched-onto-a-weapon

This rune prevents enemies from escaping your grasp by [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Fleeing|Fleeing]] to other planes. If you critically hit a target with an anchoring weapon, the weapon casts [[Spells/Planar Tether|Planar Tether]] on the target (DC 27 Will, counteract modifier +17).
