# Detecting Creatures

|  |  |
| ---- | ---- |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]]** | A creature you're observed by knows where you are and can target you normally. |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]]** | A creature that you're concealed from must succeed at a DC 5 flat check when targeting you with a non-area effect. |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]]** | A creature you're hidden from knows the space you're in. It is off-guard to you, and must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to affect you. You can Hide to become hidden, and Seek to find hidden creatures. |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]]** | When you are undetected by a creature, it's off-guard to you, can't see you, has no idea what space you occupy, and can't target you. It can try to guess your square by picking a square and attempting an attack. This works like targeting a hidden creature, but the flat check and attack roll are rolled in secret by the GM. |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Unnoticed]]** | A creature you're unnoticed by is totally unaware of your presence. |
| **[[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Invisible]]** | You're undetected by everyone. You can't become observed while invisible except via special abilities or magic. |

There are three conditions that measure the degree to which you can sense a creature: [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]], [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]], and [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]]. However, the [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]] and [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Invisible]] conditions can partially mask a creature, and the [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Unnoticed]] condition indicates you have no idea a creature is around.

With the exception of invisible, these conditions are relative to the viewer—it's possible for a creature to be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]] to you but [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] from your ally. When you're trying to target a creature that's hard to see or otherwise sense, various drawbacks apply. Most of these rules apply to objects you're trying to detect as well as creatures.

Typically, the GM tracks how well creatures detect each other, since neither party has perfect information. For example, you might think a creature is in the last place you sensed it, but it was able to [[Sneak]] away. Or you might think a creature can't see you in the dark, but it has darkvision.

You can attempt to avoid detection by using the Stealth skill to [[Avoid Notice]], [[Hide]], or [[Sneak]], or by using Deception to [[Create a Diversion]].

### Observed

In most circumstances, you can sense creatures without difficulty and target them normally. Creatures in this state are [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]]. Observing requires a precise sense, which for most creatures means sight, but see the [[Detecting with Other Senses]] sidebar for advice regarding creatures that don't use sight as their primary sense. If you can't observe the creature, it's either [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]], [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]], or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Unnoticed]], and you'll need to factor in the targeting restrictions. Even if a creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]], it might still be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]].

### Hidden

A creature that's hidden is only barely perceptible. You know what space a hidden creature occupies, but little else. Perhaps the creature just moved behind cover and successfully used the Hide action. Your target might be in a deep fogbank or behind a waterfall, where you can see some movement but can't determine an exact location. Maybe you've been [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Blinded]] or the creature is under the effects of _[[Invisibility]]_, but you used the [[Seek]] basic action to determine its general location based on hearing alone. Regardless of the specifics, you're [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard]] to a [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] creature.

When targeting a [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] creature, before you roll to determine your effect, you must attempt a DC 11 flat check. If you fail, you don't affect the creature, though the actions you used are still expended—as well as any spell slots, costs, and other resources. You remain off-guard to the creature, whether you successfully target it or not.

### Undetected

If a creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]], you don't know what space it occupies, you're [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard]] to it, and you can't easily target it. Using the [[Seek]] basic action can help you find an [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] creature, usually making it [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] from you instead of [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]]. If a creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]], that doesn't necessarily mean you're unaware of its presence—you might suspect an [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] creature is in the room with you, even though you're unable to find its space. The unnoticed condition covers creatures you're entirely unaware of.

Targeting an [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] creature is difficult. If you suspect there's a creature around, you can pick a square and attempt an attack. This works like targeting a [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] creature, but the flat check and attack roll are both rolled in secret by the GM. The GM won't tell you why you missed—whether it was due to failing the flat check, rolling an insufficient attack roll, or choosing the wrong square. The GM might allow you to try targeting an undetected creature with some spells or other abilities in a similar fashion. [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] creatures are subject to area effects normally.

For instance, suppose an enemy elf wizard cast _[[Invisibility]]_ and then [[Sneak|Sneaked]] away. You suspect that with the elf's Speed of 30 feet, they probably moved 15 feet toward an open door. You move up and attack a space 15 feet from where the elf started and directly on the path to the door. The GM secretly rolls an attack roll and flat check, but they know that you were not quite correct—the elf was actually in the adjacent space! The GM tells you that you missed, so you decide to make your next attack on the adjacent space, just in case. This time, it's the right space, and the GM's secret attack roll and flat check both succeed, so you hit!

### Unnoticed

If you have no idea a creature is even present, that creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Unnoticed]] by you. A creature that is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] might also be [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Unnoticed]]. This condition usually matters for abilities that can be used only against targets totally unaware of your presence.
