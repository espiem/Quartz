Play after an attack or effect causes you to become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Grabbed]] or knocks you [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone]].

* * *

You roll backward from the attack, landing on your feet. Take a Step away from the source of the attack (if any), and you're no longer prone or grabbed.
