# Spell Effect: See the Unseen
![[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Spell Effects/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-see-invisibility.webp|150 lp right]]

Granted by _[[See the Unseen]]_

You can see invisible creatures as though they weren't invisible, although their features are blurred, making them [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]] and difficult to identify. You also gain a +2 status bonus to checks you make to disbelieve illusions.
