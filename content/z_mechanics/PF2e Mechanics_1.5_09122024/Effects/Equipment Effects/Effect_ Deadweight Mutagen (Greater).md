# Effect: Deadweight Mutagen (Greater)
![[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Equipment Effects/zz_asset-files/icons-consumables-potions-potion-vial-corked-labeled-purple.webp|150 lp right]]

Granted by [[Deadweight Mutagen (Greater)]]

**Benefit** You gain a +3 item bonus to Athletics checks to [[Shove]] and [[Trip]], to your Fortitude and Reflex DCs against attempts to Shove or Trip you, and to saving throws against effects that attempt to force you to move or knock you [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone]]. You can attempt to Shove or Trip creatures up to two sizes larger than you.

**Drawback** You gain the [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Encumbered]] condition and can't remove it while under the effects of the mutagen.
