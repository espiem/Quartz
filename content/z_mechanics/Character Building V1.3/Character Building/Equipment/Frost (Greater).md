# Frost (Greater)
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Equipment/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-runes-weapon-property-runes-weapon-property-runes.webp|150]]

This weapon is empowered with freezing ice. It deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, the target is also [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Slowed]] until the end of your next turn unless it succeeds at a DC 34 fortitude save.

Cold damage dealt by this weapon ignores the target's cold resistance.
