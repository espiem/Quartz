# Deathless

* * *

**Activate** r envision

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** You gain the [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Doomed]] or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Wounded]] condition

* * *

**Effect** You reduce the value of the triggering condition by 1.
