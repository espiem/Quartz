# Wounding Oil
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Equipment/zz_asset-files/icons-consumables-potions-potion-tube-corked-bat-gold-red.webp|150]]

**Activate** 1 Interact

Smearing _wounding oil_, a crimson fluid, on a weapon causes it to smell strongly of blood and gives it the benefits of the [[Wounding]] rune for 1 minute.
