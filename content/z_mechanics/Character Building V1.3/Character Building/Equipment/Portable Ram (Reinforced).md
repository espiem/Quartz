# Portable Ram (Reinforced)
![[systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-held-items-portable-ram.webp|150]]

A portable ram is a handheld, ironshod wooden beam designed to knock open doors, gates, and other similar obstacles. You gain a +2 item bonus to checks to [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Force Open]] these obstacles.
