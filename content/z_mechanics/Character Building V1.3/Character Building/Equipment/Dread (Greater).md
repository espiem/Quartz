# Dread (Greater)
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Equipment/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-runes-armor-property-runes-armor-property-runes.webp|150]]

Eerie symbols cover your armor, inspiring terror in your foes. Frightened enemies within 30 feet that can see you must attempt a DC 38 will save at the end of their turn; on a failure, the value of their [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened]] condition doesn't decrease that turn, no matter the value.
