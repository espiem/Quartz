# Horrific Visage
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/icons-magic-unholy-strike-body-explode-disintegrate.webp|150]]

You briefly transform your features into the horrific visage of a hag, striking fear into your enemies. Foes in the area must attempt a Will save.

* * *

**Success** The foe is unaffected.

**Failure** The foe is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 1]].

**Critical Failure** The foe is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 2]].

* * *

**Heightened (5th)** Foes in the area are [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 1]] on a success, [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 2]] on a failure, and [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 3]] and [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Fleeing]] for 1 round on a critical failure. They are still unaffected on a critical success.
