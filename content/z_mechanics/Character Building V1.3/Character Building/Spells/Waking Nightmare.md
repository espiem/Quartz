# Waking Nightmare
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/icons-creatures-invertebrates-spider-striped-web-red.webp|150]]

You fill the creature's mind with a terrifying vision. The target must attempt a Will save. A creature frightened by this spell takes 1 additional mental damage each time it's hit by a Strike.

* * *

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 1]].

**Failure** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 2]]. If it's asleep, it wakes up and is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Paralyzed]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 3]].

* * *

**Heightened (+1)** The mental damage increases by 1.
