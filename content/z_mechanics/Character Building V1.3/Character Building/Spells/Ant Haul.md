# Ant Haul
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/icons-creatures-invertebrates-ant-strength-green.webp|150]]

You reinforce the target's musculoskeletal system to bear more weight. The target can carry 3 more Bulk than normal before becoming [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Encumbered]] and up to a maximum of 6 more Bulk.
