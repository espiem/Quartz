# Blur
![[systems-pf2e-icons-spells-blur.webp|150]]

The target's form appears blurry. It becomes [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]]. As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to Hide or Sneak.
