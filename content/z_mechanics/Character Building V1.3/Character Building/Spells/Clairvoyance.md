# Clairvoyance
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-clairvoyance.webp|150]]

You create an [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Invisible]] floating eye at a location within range (even if it's outside your line of sight or line of effect). The eye can't move, but you can see in all directions from that point as if using your normal visual senses.
