# Flashy Disappearance
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-flashy-disappearance.webp|150]]

You create a puff of colorful smoke that quickly disperses while you become temporarily [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Invisible]]. You become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] to all creatures unless they can see invisible creatures. You Stride. At the end of your movement, if you have cover, greater cover, or concealment, attempt a stealth check to Hide. You gain a +2 status bonus to this Stealth check. The invisibility then ends, and you either become [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Observed]] or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] to creatures as determined by your check to Hide, if you made one.
