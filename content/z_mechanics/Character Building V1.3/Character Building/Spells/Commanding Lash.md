# Commanding Lash
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/icons-weapons-misc-whip-red-yellow.webp|150]]

With the threat of more pain, you compel a creature you've recently harmed. You issue a command to the target, with the effects of the spell _[[Command]]_.If the target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened]], [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied]], or taking persistent damage, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the save. Regardless of the result, the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour
