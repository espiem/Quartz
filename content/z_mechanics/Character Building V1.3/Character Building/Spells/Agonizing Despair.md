# Agonizing Despair
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-agonizing-despair.webp|150]]

Your target's mind tumbles down a deep well of dread, dwelling so intently on deep-seated fears that it's painful. The target takes 4d6 mental damage with a Will saving throw.

* * *

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes half damage and becomes [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 1]].

**Failure** The target takes full damage and becomes [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 2]].

**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and becomes [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened|Frightened 3]].

* * *

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 2d6 mental.
