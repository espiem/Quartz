# Laughing Fit
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-hideous-laughter.webp|150]]

The target is overtaken with uncontrollable laughter. It must attempt a Will save.

* * *

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is plagued with uncontrollable laughter. It can't use reactions.

**Failure** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Slowed]] and can't use reactions.

**Critical Failure** The target falls [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone]] and can't use actions or reactions for 1 round. It then takes the effects of a failure.
