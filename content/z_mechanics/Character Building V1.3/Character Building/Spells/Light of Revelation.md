# Light of Revelation
![[icons-magic-light-orb-hands-humanoid-yellow.webp|150]]

You shed a luminous aura, illuminating a 30-foot radius with bright light. You and all allies in this area gain a +1 status bonus to Perception checks to detect [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Hidden]] or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Rule References/Undetected]] creatures, as well as Hidden objects, doors, and other features.
