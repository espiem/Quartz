# Downpour
![[z_mechanics/Character Building V1.3/Character Building/Spells/zz_asset-files/systems-pf2e-icons-spells-downpour.webp|150]]

You call forth a torrential downpour, which extinguishes nonmagical flames. Creatures in the area are [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]] and gain fire resistance 10. Creatures outside the area are Concealed to those inside the area. If a creature with weakness to water ends its turn in the area, the downpour triggers its weakness.  
  

* * *

**Heightened (+1)** The fire resistance increases by 2.
