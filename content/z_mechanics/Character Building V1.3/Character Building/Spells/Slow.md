# Slow
![[icons-magic-time-hourglass-tilted-gray.webp|150]]

You dilate the flow of time around the target, slowing its actions.

* * *

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Slowed]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Slowed]] for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Slowed|Slowed 2]] for 1 minute.

* * *

**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
