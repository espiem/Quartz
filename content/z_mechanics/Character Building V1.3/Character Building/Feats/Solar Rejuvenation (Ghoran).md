# Solar Rejuvenation (Ghoran)
![[icons-sundries-books-book-red-exclamation.webp|150]]

The warmth and light of the sun gives you life. If you rest outdoors for 10 minutes during the day, you regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier × half your level. You gain this benefit in addition to any healing from [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/PF2e Journals/GM Screen/Treat Wounds]].
