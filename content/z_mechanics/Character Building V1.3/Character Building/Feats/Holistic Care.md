# Holistic Care
![[icons-sundries-books-book-red-exclamation.webp|150]]

You provide emotional and spiritual care. Add [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Frightened]], [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied]], and [[Stunned 1|Stunned]] to the list of conditions you can reduce with [[Treat Condition]]. If the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can't use Treat Condition to reduce it.
