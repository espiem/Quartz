# Distracting Feint
![[icons-sundries-books-book-red-exclamation.webp|150]]

Your [[Feint|Feints]] are far more distracting than normal. While a creature is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard]] by your [[Feint]], it also takes a –2 circumstance penalty to Perception checks and Reflex saves.
