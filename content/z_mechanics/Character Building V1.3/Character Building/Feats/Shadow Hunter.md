# Shadow Hunter
![[icons-sundries-books-book-red-exclamation.webp|150]]

You blend in to your surroundings so well that others have trouble telling you apart from the terrain. While in natural terrain, you're always [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Concealed]] from all foes if you choose to be, except for your hunted prey.
