# Head Stomp
![[icons-sundries-books-book-red-exclamation.webp|150]]

Make an unarmed melee Strike against a [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Prone]] target. If it hits, until the end of your next turn the target is [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied|Stupefied 1]] (or [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Stupefied|Stupefied 2]] on a critical hit) and [[z_mechanics/PF2e Mechanics_1.5_09122024/Effects/Conditions/Off-Guard]].
